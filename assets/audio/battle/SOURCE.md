# Arcade Drive

Original AI-generated 30-second instrumental 8-bit arcade battle loop, created with ElevenLabs eleven_text_to_sound_v2. Request recorded in arcade-drive-generation.json. WAV processed with loudness matching, an 8kHz lowpass and 8ms boundary fades. Runtime uses forward looping. No music from Nier-Hacking or NieR:Automata is used.
